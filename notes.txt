NORTH STAR — Races & "who should I vote for" (deferred; do NOT start until V0 ships)
This is the main pain point we ultimately want to solve: help a voter decide who to vote for.
[ ] Race object: current/upcoming races, where they are (state/district/seat), candidates in each.
    Candidate alignment + fundraising attach to the race.
[ ] Voter survey: a quick survey that maps the USER onto the same 20 topic axes, then
    highlights the differences between the two candidates in a race and tells them:
      - who they're more POLICY-aligned with (topic match), and
      - in fundraising terms, whose BACKERS are more likely aligned with them (donor/PAC lean).
[ ] Why this is a different game than V0 (the hard parts to plan for):
    - In-progress campaigns: need CURRENT-cycle fundraising (e.g. 2026), live/partial data,
      and CHALLENGERS — not just historical data for sitting incumbents. (Today the roster is
      incumbents only and the finance cycle is 2024.)
    - No voting history for newcomers: a first-time candidate has never been in Congress, so
      the vote-based alignment doesn't exist for them. Need an alternative position signal:
      campaign promises/platform, public statements, issue questionnaires, and donor lean.
    - So a race's alignment must blend two models: vote-record alignment for incumbents, and a
      promises/statements/donor-based model for newcomers — both projected onto the same topics.
